The background of this page is the engine, running the whole recipe: one canvas, one script tag.

<canvas id="komorebi" style="position:fixed; inset:0; width:100%; height:100%; z-index:-1"></canvas>
<script src="https://komorebi.eljojo.net/dist/komorebi.player.min.js"></script>
<script>
  Komorebi.create(document.getElementById('komorebi'),
    { params: Komorebi.PRESETS['morning 2'] });
</script>
  • A page background

    Full-bleed behind your content, cycling looks on a timer. Adds the two guards worth having (no WebGL2, blocked script) and pauses itself in a hidden tab.

  • A hero panel

    The same engine inside a rounded box instead of the viewport — it takes its size from whatever element you give it — with buttons that crossfade between looks.

  • As ES modules

    No bundle and no global: import komorebi.js and presets.js directly, and tweak a look by spreading it.

  • The editor

    Every knob, live, with tooltips explaining what each one does to the light. The fastest way to find the look you want, then copy its name.
