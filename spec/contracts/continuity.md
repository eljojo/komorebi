# Continuity

*What must hold so that nothing pops: calm, written as a contract every change of the picture answers to.*

Calm is an arbiter beside physics ([principle 2](../principles.md#2-calm-is-an-arbiter-beside-physics)). The piece never startles, and the commonest way a renderer startles is a discontinuity: a leaf that is there and then is not, a shadow that jumps a layer, a whole surface that dims as one. These contracts say where continuity is owed and how it is bought. They forbid the cut, not the event: calm can hold a distinctive moment when it follows from the scene's own causes and the quiet around it stays whole.

1. **A fractional leaf and a fractional tree fade in by coverage.** Applies to `foliage_density` and `tree_count`, both continuous. A count between integers draws the whole leaves and trees plus a marginal one whose coverage is the fraction. The fade enters every reading of that leaf: its optical depth, the coverage its ballistic shadow is built on, its wood, and its litter. Why: a count is a poor axis to sweep, and without the fade a leaf or a tree would exist at full weight the instant it existed. Owner: [`model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md), with the ballistic share in [`model/leaf.md`](../model/leaf.md).

2. **A leaf lets go over a window, not an instant.** Applies to abscission. A leaf's retained weight falls smoothly to zero over ε = 0.01 of phenology before its drop day, by the same coverage factor the marginal fade uses, so it thins in the cast and its ballistic shadow thins with it, on every path. A leaf whose weight has reached zero is removed; one still fading is on the tree. Why: in a real crown a leaf goes, and the eye notices that it has gone, not the frame in which it went. Owner: [`model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

3. **A season played live may not step wider than half a fade window.** Applies wherever the phenology is driven by a control or a clock. Each applied step is clamped to half the fade window; while the clamp binds, the season moves slower than the hand and lags the clock, and the landing still reaches the stop's exact values. Why: a step wider than the window jumps every leaf whose fade lies inside it, and the canopy thins as salt-and-pepper in one frame. Owner: [`model/season.md`](../model/season.md); the size of the failure it prevents is in [`../evidence/measurements.md#season`](../evidence/measurements.md#season).

4. **A leaf's own turn never degenerates to a step.** Applies to senescence. Each leaf turns from its own start to the common finish over a smoothstep, and the spread of starts is clamped under the span so that the last leaf still has a window. Why: a turn with no width is a cut in colour. Owner: [`model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

5. **Gates open early, never late.** Applies to the season's gates (turning, dropping). A gate opens a fade window ahead of the first leaf that could need it; an early open costs nothing, because a leaf before its window is returned exactly unchanged. Why: a late gate would skip the beginning of a fade, and a leaf would appear already part-gone. Owner: [`model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

6. **The drop is a strict progression, not a wave.** Applies to the order in which a grove's leaves turn and fall. Where light orders them, each leaf has its own day, and a group that light cannot separate is ordered by each leaf's own clock, so at full light ordering one leaf goes every equal interval of the span. Ordering the group by a spatial key (height, distance from the sun-side edge) is forbidden: it sweeps the shell as a coherent wave. Why: a surface dimming as one, or a front crossing the crown, is the kind of event calm refuses; an incoherent trickle is the season. See [`determinism.md`](determinism.md) contract 12 for the key, and principle 2 for what calm can still hold: an event that follows from the scene's own causes, like a strong gust and its long settling, is not this. Owner: [`model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

7. **A slider moves each inner leaf along its own line, by a bounded step.** Applies to `inner_leaves`. A leaf joins the older wood by a smoothstep of its own threshold over a fixed glide width, in place and in height alike, so a small step in the share moves any leaf at most a fixed fraction of its own tip-to-wood reach, and pops none. The count and area of the leaves stay the look's: the knob is a share, not more leaves. Why: a share that reassigned leaves at random would reshuffle the crown with every nudge. Owner: [`model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md).

8. **A size never pops.** Applies to leaf variety's size shift and to every continuous change of leaf size: the shift is continuous in the light rank, not stepped by the light class, and renormalised so the canopy is as full as it was. Why: a leaf that changes size by a step is the most visible pop a canopy can make. Owner: [`model/leaf.md`](../model/leaf.md).

9. **The drift phase is continuous.** Applies to the incoherent band's driver. Each leaf walks a bounded orbit of its own; the canopy is periodic in the phase and returns exactly to its starting configuration each cycle. The one hard requirement is that φ and φ + ε differ only infinitesimally, so the eye reads a morph and not a cut. Any control that instead reshuffles a set (an integer count) steps discontinuously, which is why density is the wrong knob for reorganisation. Why: the reorganising gaps are meant to read as caustics, merging and dissolving, and a reshuffle reads as a jump-cut. Owner: [`model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).

10. **Springs rest at zero, and wind carries across a regrow.** Applies to the spring hierarchy (trunk, limb, twig, leaf). Every bend rests at zero in still air, so the still image is the grown tree exactly; the independent branch forcing eases to zero as the wind does, so the springs settle. When a canopy change regrows the grove, the springs' common prefix carries over and a newly grown tree starts at rest, so the breeze does not reset mid-gust. Branches return to a stored, honest equilibrium. Why: wind that resets is a cut in time, and a still image moved by the motion system would be a change nobody made. Owner: [`model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).

11. **Transitions never cut.** Applies to every change from one look to another. A transition retains what it honestly can and reorganises rather than dissolves; where it must dissolve, the swap is hidden under the source's own bloom, capped short of saturation so the frame never floods to white. Within that:
    - **A gate at zero is crossed by fading what it restricts, never by growing it from nothing.** An aperture crossing its zero keeps the enabled end's geometry and fades its restriction; the vignette's iris sweeps its boundary in from outside the frame, or out to it, over a quarter of the route; a woodless end's wood fades out over one half and in over the other, meeting zero at the midpoint; an eclipse is crossed by the moon sliding on or off, its limb soft only while it moves.
    - **A proxy is handed off through darkness, not dropped.** When `leaf_diffuse` crosses zero and the zero end has an active scatter proxy, the proxy's light fades to zero over the first half before the diffuse light grows over the second.
    - **A different law is not reached by a near value.** Where exactly zero selects another law (the texel's reading at `leaf_diffuse` 0), a route keeps each endpoint's own value in its native cast rather than let a tiny positive value survive to the last frame and snap.
    - **Native endpoints are reached exactly.** A route lands on the target's exact parameters, and a moving cast hands the picture to the native caster through a smooth share that reaches it before landing.
    - **Reversal keeps progress.** Turning back keeps the displayed progress, the carriers, the tone response and both wind states; a request made mid-route waits for a settled frame, and the latest such request replaces an earlier one.

    Why: the arrow keys are the piece's way of moving through places, and a cut between places is the least calm thing it could do. Owner: [`model/transitions.md`](../model/transitions.md); the gate and promises of each route are [`transitions.md`](transitions.md).

12. **Auto-quality ratchets; it does not hunt.** Applies to the runtime quality governor. It drops quality when frames slip, debounced against lone spikes, and is reluctant to climb back: each forced downsize doubles the wait before it probes upward again, so it settles to a stable level and parks there. Full quality is re-probed only on an explicit re-tune. Quality levers that reallocate (the bake resolution) move only at snapped level boundaries. Why: a governor that oscillates is a visible breathing of resolution that has no cause in the world. Owner: [`gates-and-rungs.md`](gates-and-rungs.md).

## Known tensions

- **Adaptive frame-rate steps the slow shimmer.** While the canopy is nearly still, the heavy passes run at an idle rate and the frames between re-present the last one; full rate returns the instant the wind picks up, with hysteresis on the springs' own motion. The saving is large and the cost is visible stepping on the slowest drift, which this contract does not otherwise allow. It ships on as a performance baseline. This is a tension, named, not resolved; where it is weighed is [`../exploration/directions.md`](../exploration/directions.md).
- **Leaf variety re-deals every outline when it is turned on from zero.** That is crossfaded as a structural change. A canopy morph that re-ranks the light no longer swaps an outline: the class is continuous and a leaf between two classes hangs a blend of their outlines, so its lobes move with its rank (decided 2026-11-09). See [`determinism.md`](determinism.md).
- **The layer path changes a leaf's layer at a bin edge.** A leaf moving continuously in height, under `inner_leaves` or a morph, crosses into another depth layer and its shadow moves to that layer's throw. That is the depth discretisation's cost, the one approximation every frame pays; the faithful cast does not have it ([`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md)).
- **The tie-break does not reach the absolute light order.** At `exposure_absolute` 1 the up-sun shell is one value and still turns and falls together: the coherent surface contract 6 forbids, allowed by a knob that chooses raw light over rank. See [`determinism.md`](determinism.md).
- **A dissolve is still a swap.** Where a topology change cannot reorganise, one grove replaces another between two frames under the bloom. Luminance cannot see it; churn can. Routes that reorganise are replacing it route by route ([`../model/transitions.md`](../model/transitions.md)); measurements of the bloom's cap are in [`../evidence/measurements.md#transitions`](../evidence/measurements.md#transitions).
- **The season's clamp lags the hand.** When a season is played faster than half a fade window a step, the picture runs behind the control. The lag is chosen over the pop.
