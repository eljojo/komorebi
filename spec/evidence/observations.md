[`../experience.md`](../experience.md) tells the experience as one account. This file keeps its sources: his words as he said them, where and when, and the choices that followed. ## Observations

### The park, early spring (the founding afternoon)

Under the trees in a park, the leaves not yet fully grown, the canopy sparse. The dapples on the ground were **soft, out of focus**, "like looking through an old prime lens, the kind with character", and the focus was not fixed: thick and thin clouds drifted across the sun and the whole pattern **breathed in and out of focus**. "The bokeh on the ground was the thing — that very out-of-focus, glowing, low-contrast quality."

The big soft light was seen, there in the park, to be literally an accumulation of many smaller ones; the many-suns method was chosen because it matches that perception.

Two winds were remembered vividly:

- **A strong directional breeze** pushed the leaves to one side and the gaps **translated almost perfectly**. "You could feel the breathing of the wind," an easing-in rather than a switch. At a gust's peak, "the elasticity of the branches fighting the wind", and when it died they returned to their starting point, with a **backlash**: bent hard against their limit, the branches snapped back past rest and settled.
- **A very faint wind, in all directions at once**, "the kind that seems to come up when the sun shines a little stronger". Nothing translated; the leaves **rearranged**, opening new gaps, because sparse leaves still mattered one by one. It looked "unmistakably, like water caustics moving across the floor of a pool as waves collapse".

Under the faintest breeze the shapes **glistened**, a fine twinkling in place.

Beneath it, **calm**: "the mindfulness of lying under the trees — peace, an innate selfness, the oneness with nature."

Date: not recorded. Carried by [`../experience.md`](../experience.md), [`../model/source.md`](../model/source.md), [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).

### Through glass

"A later observation, brought in from looking at glass": through some lenses the bright and dark edges fringe, the red and blue of a highlight splitting apart. It became leaf-edge diffraction, the prism. Date not recorded. Carried by [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md).

### The bedroom curtain

Sunset through a backyard tree onto a moss-green velvet drape, seen from the bed: dapples standing up a vertical cloth lit from behind, the window's bars sharp near the glass, the tree's sun-images soft, the cloth glowing dimly from within. Of its priority (2026-10-07 interview): "for curtain i care a bit less about, maybe that one you can put on hold until the end of the phases?" (from the bot's log, uncommitted source). Carried by [`../model/receivers.md`](../model/receivers.md).

### The tent

Komorebi on the nylon of a tent pitched under trees at midday, photographed from inside, lying down: canopy overhead, light falling onto the panels around you, and *different panels at different brightnesses, because they face different ways*. Some patches read as hard bright spots, others as diffuse glow: the spotlight-versus-haze contrast that motivates the lateral diffusion tier. Lying in a 2P with the feet toward the far end, the seam runs away down the middle of the ceiling, forks just short of the end, and the bright vent triangle sits in the fork, apex up; the same behind you. Date not recorded. Carried by [`../model/receivers.md`](../model/receivers.md).

### The hammock

Lying back in a hammock at a festival: tall trunks converging overhead, crowns lit from behind against the bright sky, the whole grove leaning together with the gusts. Looking into the sun through a backlit canopy: "it'd be easy to look at the sun and the canopy would act like sunglasses but still leaks would leak through." Date not recorded. Carried by [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).

### The projector in the yard

José throws the piece onto his backyard at night with a real projector (a TOPTRO A2, from the bot's log, uncommitted source). A projector is a hard rectangle; nothing in a canopy has a frame; the light should pool in the yard. From the projector session of 2026-10-08 (all from the bot's log, uncommitted source): his eight projector looks were authored under a projector gamma of 2.6 and the projector also runs at 1.8, where the red and cherry family may read too hard; he dislikes the background colour (the "background-colour gripe"); and one proposal took its aim from his words, "the background dark and only the light going thru the trees". Photographs of the projection are in `reference-photos-do-not-commit/outside-projection/`. Carried by [`../model/camera.md`](../model/camera.md), [`../contracts/looks.md`](../contracts/looks.md).

### The fall

The trees were watched through a fall (2026-10).

- **The colour of the trees** (2026-10-08): "something that matters a lot to me is the colour of the trees, how some trees are red while the one next to it is yellow or green, why that happens, how they're organized in nature and how is information scattered or grouped." From his photographs: the species sets palette and clock; within a species an individual shifts by days to two weeks (σ 0.03–0.06 of the season, with a long tail for stressed trees) and its red depth runs about 2:1 between neighbours; within a crown it is exposure. One tree carries green, gold and red side by side, with about a 3:1 range in red depth across its leaves. Carried by [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **Shade orders the turn:** "trees and buildings cast shadow onto each other which determine which gets red first"; tips red going through yellow to green at the bottom of the same tree, by access to light. Date not recorded.
- **Letting go:** one all-yellow tree had half its leaves on the ground by the end of ten days, the sunlit ones first. The beech keeps about half its leaves, brown, on the tree through the season (marcescence); "half of its leaves were on the ground" by the end of ten days; the beech's leaves go brown, and then go. Date not recorded.
- **Rain:** "one day after rain many leaves fell on the ground." The carpet lay darker and more saturated wet. Date not recorded.
- **A fallen leaf:** a backlit red leaf glows red; the same leaf lying on the ground, lit from above, reads tan-brown and desaturated (his photo of a lobe curled away from the window).
- **The grammar under the trees:** "trunk, branch, twig, leave" — the same grammar all the way down to the leaf.
- **The leaf held to the sun:** "it looks like a maze… cells with white walls in between… like lizard skin. the colour i dig too, the darkening of it."
- **Looking down at a crown** (y4, park 1, sun 70°): it "looked off, like it's broken … I don't think it'd look like that irl." And of the floor camera's tree: "without roots the tree looks like it's floating" (2026-10-07, from the bot's log, uncommitted source).
- **Leaves that look alike** (2026-10-09): "we need much more leaf diversity, more species and more variability within species, i showed you so many different maple leaves from my reference photos and looking at those sheets, they all look the same to me." Of his ultraviolet maple: "last night i actually tried rendering maple and it looked so thin and weird."
- **Frosted or stained** (2026-10-07 interview, from the bot's log, uncommitted source): "i like the dark patches of the left one… i'd like to try the blend, stained glass probably is the ultimate goal but things might be missing that get in its way. in reality, the feel i get from the forest changes depending on how thin/thick the canopy is and how bright the day is, too. some types of sunlight seem to cause more of one effect than the other, and i guess if the leaves are wet or not i wonder if it influences too."
- **"A redhead"** (same interview): "let's build it based on park1 or something, i can get the fall feeling there much more easily. the other looks feel like 'a redhead'": too uniformly red, too saturated, the shade too red (from the bot's log, uncommitted source).

## Reference photographs

All of these live in `reference-photos-do-not-commit/`, which is untracked and must stay so. File names are given where the record names them.

- **The thirteen maples.** DSC_0617–0622, DSCF1667 (three leaves), DSC_0656 (five leaves): sugar maples, measured as the engine's leaves are (radial profile from a hand-placed petiole base, area-normalised). - **`leaf-maze-crop.png`.** The areole maze crop the held leaf's maze was compared with: T-junctions at near right angles, cells darkening toward their centres (not modelled).
- **Photo 0631** (`IMG_0631.jpeg`). One fully orange sugar maple among green-yellow neighbours of its own kind: the picture the per-tree clock exists to draw.
- **The beech.** The tree that keeps half its leaves, brown. A gold, half-down tree, `IMG_0404`, became the proposal "the beech" and then the look `the yellow tree` (from the bot's log, uncommitted source).
- **The ride photos of bare ground.** Lit/shade about 2 to 2.6 under a 12° sun and about 6 to 8 under 45°; the engine's comparison is in [measurements](measurements.md#light). An analysis folder `ride-analysis-2026-10-08/` sits beside them; the old spec does not cite it.
- **The leaf held to the window / the curled lobe.** A lobe curled away from the window, lit from the room side: tan-brown and desaturated, the evidence that a fallen leaf is seen by reflection.
- **The tent reference.** The photograph from inside a tent at midday: panels at different brightnesses, spotlight against haze. File not recorded.
- **The pressed leaves — "not these leaves".** The first maple (the five-pointed star) was "calibrated on José's pressed leaves", which were not the thirteen; by the thirteen's measure it resembled none of them. See [dead-ends](dead-ends.md#leaf).
- **The 2026-10-08 leaf batch.** Elm, maple and sumac leaves photographed backlit and measured (report and scripts in `leaf-analysis-2026-10-08/`). None were wetted: they were fresh, still-moist leaves off the ground, so the batch is fresh litter, not a wet/dry pair. Yellow blades measured warmer than the engine's gold, and the elm's brown blotches deeper than the provisional tannin (from the bot's log, uncommitted source).
- **Species photographs.** Sixteen species identified in his photos for a later round: sugar maple in 18 frames, elm 6, buckeye 4, Amur maple, red and Freeman maple, pines, spruce, sumac, creeper, birch, cherry, red oak, silver maple, cedar (from the bot's log, uncommitted source).
- **The yard projection.** `outside-projection/` (photographs of the piece on the yard, and his eight projector looks as JSON in `presets/`) and `projector-fit-2026-10-08/` (from the bot's log, uncommitted source, for the presets folder).
- **Not yet taken: the grey card.** The same leaves backlit, then dry and wet beside a grey card in the Gatineau, would fix κ, the cuticle share and wetness's scale. See [measurements](measurements.md#season).
