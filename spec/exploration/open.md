# What is open

*Everything uncertain, unbuilt or waiting on a decision, with its status and the part of the model it would change.*

Each item names a status:

- **[accepted, unfinished]** — the spec names it as the direction, and a gate or hook for it exists (or the item says plainly that none does yet).
- **[idea]** — proposed or named as a possibility, not chosen.
- **[provisional value]** — a number in use that awaits a measurement.
- **[José's call]** — the decision is the artist's, and is waiting on him.
- **[open]** — unexplained or undecided, and a model file lists it as Open.
- **[superseded]** — answered, or turned into a knob; it stays here only so the question is not asked again.

Items marked *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)* come from the working notes of the overnight sessions; they carry no more authority than a question asked. The artist's larger directions for the piece are in [`directions.md`](directions.md).

## the original open questions

The founding seven questions were left deliberately unresolved, to be tuned in the making. Most are now knobs; where a model file still lists one as Open, it stays open here.

- **Skeleton depth** · **[open]** · How few branching levels still give convincing multi-scale gaps. Built so far: depth is the knob `branch_levels`, with `branch_children` and the species bundles setting it per look, and multi-scale gaps fall out of the skeleton's self-similarity. `model/canopy.md` still lists this, the default foliage density and the layer count as Open. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md).
- **Source-sample count, the core/halo split and the halo's falloff** · **[open]** · Built so far: the sample count is a machine setting (`sample_count`, session ceilings with optional look requests) that the auto-quality governor trims; the split is the cloud knob's redistribution of weight from core to halo. The halo is flat because the sampler's is, and the sky view shows that same flat aureole on purpose. How many samples a look needs against what it costs, and the true falloff, are Open in the model. [`../model/source.md`](../model/source.md).
- **The default foliage density, and how it is exposed** · **[open]** · Built so far: `foliage_density` is a first-class per-look knob that sets the twinkle ↔ reorganisation regime, with each clump on its own seed and the marginal leaf faded in so that it sweeps without jumping; a summer density change morphs the same grove. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md), [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **The stiffening curve and the damping that make the backlash read right** · **[open]** · Built as a restoring force that diverges at a ceiling with damping cut on the return stroke, exposed as `sway_stiffness`, `sway_ceiling`, `damping_ratio` and `backlash_gain`. Whether today's tuning reads right is the question the model still marks Open, and it returns as the too-breathy wind, below. [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **Layer count and per-layer height assignment** · **[open]** · Built so far: `layer_count` (3–5) is a knob, and leaves bin to layers by the height they actually grew to, through the crown's own curve. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md).
- **How the two wind bands are authored** · **[open]** for the coherent band's noise type, correlation length and spectra, **[accepted, unfinished]** for the incoherent one · The coherent band is built: broadband forcing over octaves with a −5/3 slope, named patterns, gustiness, a crosswind channel and a slow weather layer. The incoherent band is still a drift phase advanced by hand or by a clock; the wind driving it is the open half (*the incoherent band, driven by the wind*, under motion). [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **The tone-mapping curve, and how exposure and contrast couple to source width** · **[open]** · Built so far: the tone map is a per-look choice (none, Reinhard, ACES) applied last on linear light; exposure is the free constant each look sits, the one the cosine law leaves to the author; contrast sag under cloud is emergent from the source, not a coupling. A coupling is built (2026-11-10): `cloud_exposure` brightens the whole frame as the cloud thickens, exposure × (1 + cloud), each look re-sat to show as before at its own cloud, so the frame opens up under a passing cloud and the shadows keep their depth. **Reconsider later:** a version that also greys toward flat only in real overcast (contrast × (1 − ½·smoothstep(0.7, 1, cloud)), exposure's lift capped at 0.7) reads as more real, but risks flat grey frames on a projector; it is `archive/reviews/2026-11-09-item35-clouds-passing`'s D. A video of four couplings while clouds pass is `archive/reviews/2026-11-09-item35-clouds-passing`; its D is exposure × (1 + min(c, 0.7)) and contrast × (1 − ½·smoothstep(0.7, 1, c)). `model/camera.md` still marks whether exposure and contrast should follow the source's width as Open. [`../model/camera.md`](../model/camera.md), [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md).

## light

- **The skylight's level against the beam** · **[José's call]** · José's photographs of bare ground read lit/shade about 2–2.6 under a 12° sun and 6–8 under 45°. With the cosine law the engine's change with elevation matches his, but the level is too contrasty at every elevation, because the shade's skylight is an authored level that does not fall with the sun. The skylight a look wants is its author's to set; whether to set it from his photographs is his to decide. A skylight about three times stronger would land both numbers, by arithmetic, not by render *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)*. Seen on a sheet on 2026-11-09 (×1 to ×3 on `montebello` and `park 1` at 12° and 45°): he liked the shipped level and also some raised ones, and could not name one level for every look; held open, likely a per-look choice. [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md), [`../model/fill.md`](../model/fill.md); [`../evidence/measurements.md#light`](../evidence/measurements.md#light).

[`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md), [`../model/fill.md`](../model/fill.md), [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).

## season

- **The patchy turn** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · The rest of the species step besides the oak: a turn that comes in patches across a crown. It waits on José's judging of the maple sheets. Whatever later shapes the turn clock also shapes the order inside a tie of equal light, and the patchy turn must weigh that against calm. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The integrated season sun** · **[idea]** · Exposure is measured along one representative ray, 45° from the south, for every look. A stand at another latitude or on a slope wants another (one constant). The physical successor is a season's sun integrated over more than one direction, which would shade most of the up-sun face and re-rank every leaf; it would also dissolve the one tie the tie-break cannot reach, where the raw light is literally equal at `exposure_absolute` 1. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **`groundShade`** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · A named stub that returns 1: the seam where the valley's terrain (a hill's shadow, a slope turned from the sun, the building across the way) will multiply its own sun-visibility into each leaf's exposure, before the rank. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The site field (P6)** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · Trees are independent; real neighbours correlate by site (soil, water, the cold pocket). The valley's site field is named as where that lives. No hook exists yet beyond each tree's own draws. A look that wants a real tail of stressed trees also wants more than five of them. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **Rain as a writer of wetness and dirt (P8)** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · `leaf_wetness` darkens and saturates the carpet, and was made a live, morphing key precisely so that a rain writer can drive it. What waits is the writer itself: rain that raises the wetness, lowers the dirt with it (today an author of a rain look darkens the floor albedo (`ground_r`, `ground_g`, `ground_b`) by hand, because wet leaves on dry dirt raise the contrast a real rain day lowers), and a wet leaf's gloss, which a Lambertian floor cannot show. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md), [`../model/receivers.md`](../model/receivers.md).
- **The pigment basis** · **[provisional value]** · The optical depths of carotenoid, anthocyanin and tannin are fitted to a leaf model, PROSPECT-D, over the measured autumn-maple range, not to José's leaves. Settled by one backlit leaf per pigment state against a diffuser, τ = −ln(leaf / diffuser). [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md), [`../model/leaf.md`](../model/leaf.md).
- **The gold past the gamut** · **[open]** · A real carotenoid leaf's transmitted yellow lies outside sRGB: its red is above the light's own and, deep enough, its blue below zero. A depth that never adds light cannot paint it, so the engine's gold is less saturated than the real one, and the basis's largest error sits there. A wider working space would reach it. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The carpet's colour: κ, and the litter's age** · **[provisional value]** · The fallen leaf's reflectance (Kubelka–Munk) has one unknown, κ, set at 2 by José's judgement from sheets; `litter_age` 0.15 is provisional too. Settled by photographing the same leaves backlit (for τ), then dry and wet beside a grey card in the Gatineau, which fits κ, the cuticle's share and wetness's effective scale. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The tree's lead inside a tie** · **[idea]** · Inside an exact tie of light the order is each leaf's own clock, not its tree's: an early tree's leaf and a late tree's leaf of equal light are ranked by their own draws, their days differing by the leads on top. Named, not fixed. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The shell's shared day at full absolute light** · **[idea]** · With `exposure_absolute` 1 and `turn_exposure` 1 the unshaded shell sits at one rank and so one turn and one drop day: a named corner, not fixed. The integrated season sun, above, is its physical cure. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The spring's shape** · **[idea]** · Leaf-out grows each leaf by scaling it; a young leaf's limpness, hairs and its own diffuse share, the shoot's elongation and a vigorous shoot's smaller late leaves are not modelled; whether a tree that turns early also leafs out early is not known (the default is no). [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md#leaf-out-the-spring-half).
- **Falling as an event** · **[idea]** · Leaves vanish where they hang and are laid where the season's wind would have carried them: there is no fall through the air, no gust that shakes a tree bare, no rain day as a cause. (The travel routes between floor and canopy fly leaves, but as a transition grammar, not a season.) The carpet's stillness is José's decision and is not open. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md); see [`directions.md`](directions.md#distinctive-events-within-calm).
- **The season spectrum** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · José chose the spectrum, casualties and all ("i like the option of 'the spectrum', the one with casualties", 2026-11-09, from `archive/reviews/2026-11-09-item9-season-spectrum`). A design, nothing built: a year's leaf loss as one curve, shaped by a named year character (good, dry, late, lean) at a vigour, with the summer's casualties lying brown under the fall's leaves. Six decisions are his, the largest being whether the season slider's summer half shows casualties on every floor look. A named gap beside it: the casualties land at the trunk's foot, where his photographs show bare soil under the crown *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)*. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

## sky-view

- **The tree as a shaded body** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · Looking up, the tree is an object made of shadow: connected, tapering, dark, and unlit. The floor camera now sees it lit (`tree_view`): leaves lit as receivers, a two-faced leaf with its sheen, measured bark. The sky view can take the same leaf and bark; it has not. [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md), [`../model/camera.md`](../model/camera.md#the-tree-seen-from-the-floor-camera).
- **The leaf's sheen** · **[open]** · A sunlit crown seen toward the sun glints. Drawn as one Beckmann lobe on a flat leaf (Bousquet's beech: n 1.56, σ 0.29; its peak, about 0.34 sr⁻¹ at 60°, against the 0.29 measured) the crown from 12 m read as snow: whole leaves flashed white. With the blade undulating (slopes to 25°) and the lobe widened by each pixel's spread of normals it still did, as a finer white speckle; from under the tree, into the sun, either was fine. Not yet known: how much of a real leaf glints at once at that distance, and whether the tail's shoulder whitens every glint. The leaf ships without it. [`../model/leaf.md`](../model/leaf.md#a-leaf-seen).
- **The tree view's reach** · **[open]** · The floor camera tilts at most 80° and zooms to 40 m, so at a person's 1.6 m eye it sees about 35° above the horizon: a small tree a few metres off whole, its crown overhead not. Whether the floor camera looks further up, or that view stays the sky view's, is the artist's. [`../model/camera.md`](../model/camera.md#open).
- **The leaf glow under the cosine law** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · By the cosine law's own argument the lantern's scattered sun should take the leaf's `sin el` while the seen disk must not; both read the sun's colour inside the shared ray, so separating them needs the ray split in two. Until then the sky view takes no factor. [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md), [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md).
- **The leaked disk's own glare** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · The disk seen through the gaps is composed last and kept apart from the glow for one reason: a later phase, the eye's, will take it out of the frame and give it a glare of its own. Until then the whole-frame veiling glare stands in. [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).
- **The wood's silhouette edge** · **[idea]** · The wood gets one constant angular soft edge. Physically it should be soft by the sun's angle only where it stands against the source, and knife-sharp against blue sky; knowing which needs the source's angular coverage at the wood's own position, a second ray march. [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).
- **Multiple scattering** · **[idea]** · The glow is single scatter, so a dense crown is darker looking up than in life, where light bounced twice inside the canopy is most of what fills it. Closed for the floor: the crown's own bounce reaches it at most at 0.15 % of the sun, and the ground's is built ([`../model/fill.md`](../model/fill.md)). Open for the sky view, where a lit crown is what the eye sees. [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).
- **Undersides lit from below, looking up** · **[idea]** · The floor takes the ground the undersides return (`ground_bounce`); the sky view does not, so the shade below and the crown above answer differently. - **The incoherent band, driven by the wind** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · The fine drift is a phase advanced by hand or by a clock; when the incoherent wind band is built it was to become the driver of that phase. A prototype (2026-11-09, `archive/reviews/2026-11-09-item16-incoherent-band`) found two things: the flutter rock is already an incoherent forcing on orientation, one frequency, scaled by the wind, so the band is partly built; and a band driven by the gusts gives up the drift's exact periodic return, which is a law of [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md) today: the canopy would return only to rest. Built since (2026-11-15): under `wind_air` the flutter follows a gust carried through the grove with eddies from place to place, and under `wind_depth` only as deep into a crown as the wind reaches (*The air among the leaves*); both drive the flutter's strength only, and the drift phase is still not driven. - **The routes that still dissolve** · **[idea]** · The crossfade's gate is narrow and every condition in it is something it cannot yet do: a faithful end (one grove pre-integrated into one texture, nothing to blend against) and a mode flip (a second scene, not a second grove). Two overhead faithful groves now travel their leaves; faithful pairs with wood, a standing scene, a receiver or the carpet still take the scene dissolve. [`../model/transitions.md`](../model/transitions.md).
- **The freeze where a switch starts** · **[idea]** · A dissolve's first frame is about 51 ms warm on an M4 at 1080p (montebello → afternoon 6), and a season travel's about 52 ms (montebello → park fall). Neither has one large cost left; each is a few pieces of 4–12 ms and the GPU's own work ([`../evidence/measurements.md`](../evidence/measurements.md#a-switchs-first-frame-warm)). Growing the incoming grove on a thread of its own would take about 8 ms off the dissolve's, for a worker the bundles would have to carry. Shaders are not the cost: a background warm-up measured nothing ([`../evidence/measurements.md`](../evidence/measurements.md#the-dissolves-outgoing-grove)). The canopy morph (`prism → morning 3b`) regrows the grove every frame of the route, and since the build stopped redrawing its numbers it holds about 17 ms a frame at 1080p on an M4 ([`../evidence/measurements.md`](../evidence/measurements.md#the-groves-build)). [`../model/transitions.md`](../model/transitions.md).
- **Exposure across a change of camera** · **[open]** · A route that lands on the floor is blended in the floor's law for its whole length, whatever camera it left; between two looks on one camera it is that camera's law; any other change of camera is the plain tween of exposure. The floor's law is right only for the half of the route the floor camera draws: the dapples' level there is exposure times the beam's factor, and the blend keeps that product on a straight line between the two ends. Unexplained is what the other half should do. Each half could be blended in the law of the camera that draws it, and the level then has to meet at the swap; the plain tween is no answer, since it overshoots. On `tent 1 → acid dreams` and `canopy 2 → acid dreams` the plain tween takes the midpoint frame to a luma of 243 and 247 of 255 against 224; the floor's law dips below both ends on `curtain 2 → afternoon 4b` (exposure 0.77 at the midpoint, the ends about 1.57 and 1.0). Evidence: `archive/reviews/2026-11-17-same-camera-exposure/`. [`../model/transitions.md`](../model/transitions.md#live-morph).
