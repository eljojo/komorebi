# What is open

*Everything uncertain, unbuilt or waiting on a decision, with its status and the part of the model it would change.*

Each item names a status:

- **[accepted, unfinished]** — the spec names it as the direction, and a gate or hook for it exists (or the item says plainly that none does yet).
- **[idea]** — proposed or named as a possibility, not chosen.
- **[provisional value]** — a number in use that awaits a measurement.
- **[José's call]** — the decision is the artist's, and is waiting on him.
- **[open]** — unexplained or undecided, and a model file lists it as Open.
- **[superseded]** — answered, or turned into a knob; it stays here only so the question is not asked again.

Items marked *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)* come from the working notes of the overnight sessions; they carry no more authority than a question asked. The artist's larger directions for the piece are in [`directions.md`](directions.md).

## the original open questions

The founding seven questions were left deliberately unresolved, to be tuned in the making. Most are now knobs; where a model file still lists one as Open, it stays open here.

- **Skeleton depth** · **[open]** · How few branching levels still give convincing multi-scale gaps. Built so far: depth is the knob `branch_levels`, with `branch_children` and the species bundles setting it per look, and multi-scale gaps fall out of the skeleton's self-similarity. `model/canopy.md` still lists this, the default foliage density and the layer count as Open. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md).
- **Source-sample count, the core/halo split and the halo's falloff** · **[open]** · Built so far: the sample count is a machine setting (`sample_count`, the Tune panel) that the auto-quality governor trims and the profiler measures; the split is the cloud knob's redistribution of weight from core to halo. The halo is flat because the sampler's is, and the sky view shows that same flat aureole on purpose. How many samples a look needs against what it costs, and the true falloff, are Open in the model. [`../model/source.md`](../model/source.md).
- **The default foliage density, and how it is exposed** · **[open]** · Built so far: `foliage_density` is a first-class per-look knob that sets the twinkle ↔ reorganisation regime, with each clump on its own seed and the marginal leaf faded in so that it sweeps without jumping; a summer density change morphs the same grove. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md), [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **The stiffening curve and the damping that make the backlash read right** · **[open]** · Built as a restoring force that diverges at a ceiling with damping cut on the return stroke, exposed as `sway_stiffness`, `sway_ceiling`, `damping_ratio` and `backlash_gain`. Whether today's tuning reads right is the question the model still marks Open, and it returns as the too-breathy wind, below. [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **Layer count and per-layer height assignment** · **[open]** · Built so far: `layer_count` (3–5) is a knob, and leaves bin to layers by the height they actually grew to, through the crown's own curve. [`../model/canopy.md`](../model/canopy.md).
- **How the two wind bands are authored** · **[open]** for the coherent band's noise type, correlation length and spectra, **[accepted, unfinished]** for the incoherent one · The coherent band is built: broadband forcing over octaves with a −5/3 slope, named patterns, gustiness, a crosswind channel and a slow weather layer. The incoherent band is still a drift phase advanced by hand or by a clock; the wind driving it is the open half (*the incoherent band, driven by the wind*, under motion). [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md).
- **The tone-mapping curve, and how exposure and contrast couple to source width** · **[open]** · Built so far: the tone map is a per-look choice (none, Reinhard, ACES) applied last on linear light; exposure is the free constant each look sits, the one the cosine law leaves to the author; contrast sag under cloud is emergent from the source, not a coupling. A coupling is built (2026-11-10): `cloud_exposure` brightens the whole frame as the cloud thickens, exposure × (1 + cloud), each look re-sat to show as before at its own cloud, so the frame opens up under a passing cloud and the shadows keep their depth. **Reconsider later:** a version that also greys toward flat only in real overcast (contrast × (1 − ½·smoothstep(0.7, 1, cloud)), exposure's lift capped at 0.7) reads as more real, but risks flat grey frames on a projector; it is `test-gl/out/review/2026-11-09-item35-clouds-passing`'s D. A video of four couplings while clouds pass is `test-gl/out/review/2026-11-09-item35-clouds-passing`; its D is exposure × (1 + min(c, 0.7)) and contrast × (1 − ½·smoothstep(0.7, 1, c)). `model/camera.md` still marks whether exposure and contrast should follow the source's width as Open. [`../model/camera.md`](../model/camera.md), [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md).

## light

- **The skylight's level against the beam** · **[José's call]** · José's photographs of bare ground read lit/shade about 2–2.6 under a 12° sun and 6–8 under 45°. With the cosine law the engine's change with elevation matches his, but the level is too contrasty at every elevation, because the shade's skylight is an authored level that does not fall with the sun. The skylight a look wants is its author's to set; whether to set it from his photographs is his to decide. A skylight about three times stronger would land both numbers, by arithmetic, not by render *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)*. Seen on a sheet on 2026-11-09 (×1 to ×3 on `montebello` and `park 1` at 12° and 45°): he liked the shipped level and also some raised ones, and could not name one level for every look; held open, likely a per-look choice. [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md), [`../model/fill.md`](../model/fill.md); [`../evidence/measurements.md#light`](../evidence/measurements.md#light).

A real gradient wants a sky model the engine does not have; a screen-space ramp would be painting and is refused. [`../model/light.md`](../model/light.md), [`../model/fill.md`](../model/fill.md), [`../model/sky-view.md`](../model/sky-view.md).

## season

- **The patchy turn** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · The rest of the species step besides the oak: a turn that comes in patches across a crown. It waits on José's judging of the maple sheets. Whatever later shapes the turn clock also shapes the order inside a tie of equal light, and the patchy turn must weigh that against calm. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The integrated season sun** · **[idea]** · Exposure is measured along one representative ray, 45° from the south, for every look. A stand at another latitude or on a slope wants another (one constant). The physical successor is a season's sun integrated over more than one direction, which would shade most of the up-sun face and re-rank every leaf; it would also dissolve the one tie the tie-break cannot reach, where the raw light is literally equal at `exposure_absolute` 1. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **`groundShade`** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · A named stub that returns 1: the seam where the valley's terrain (a hill's shadow, a slope turned from the sun, the building across the way) will multiply its own sun-visibility into each leaf's exposure, before the rank. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The site field (P6)** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · Trees are independent; real neighbours correlate by site (soil, water, the cold pocket). The valley's site field is named as where that lives. No hook exists yet beyond each tree's own draws. A look that wants a real tail of stressed trees also wants more than five of them. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **Rain as a writer of wetness and dirt (P8)** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · `leaf_wetness` darkens and saturates the carpet, and was made a live, morphing key precisely so that a rain writer can drive it. What waits is the writer itself: rain that raises the wetness, lowers the dirt with it (today an author of a rain look darkens the floor albedo (`ground_r`, `ground_g`, `ground_b`) by hand, because wet leaves on dry dirt raise the contrast a real rain day lowers), and a wet leaf's gloss, which a Lambertian floor cannot show. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md), [`../model/receivers.md`](../model/receivers.md).
- **The pigment basis** · **[provisional value]** · The optical depths of carotenoid, anthocyanin and tannin are chosen from the pigments' known absorption, not fitted. Settled by one backlit leaf per pigment state against a diffuser, τ = −ln(leaf / diffuser). Until then their ordering is what is held. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md), [`../model/leaf.md`](../model/leaf.md).
- **The carpet's colour: κ, and the litter's age** · **[provisional value]** · The fallen leaf's reflectance (Kubelka–Munk) has one unknown, κ, set at 2 by José's judgement from sheets; `litter_age` 0.15 is provisional too. Settled by photographing the same leaves backlit (for τ), then dry and wet beside a grey card in the Gatineau, which fits κ, the cuticle's share and wetness's effective scale. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md); [`../evidence/observations.md#decisions`](../evidence/observations.md#decisions).
- **The tree's lead inside a tie** · **[idea]** · Inside an exact tie of light the order is each leaf's own clock, not its tree's: an early tree's leaf and a late tree's leaf of equal light are ranked by their own draws, their days differing by the leads on top. Named, not fixed. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **The shell's shared day at full absolute light** · **[idea]** · With `exposure_absolute` 1 and `turn_exposure` 1 the unshaded shell sits at one rank and so one turn and one drop day: a named corner, not fixed. The integrated season sun, above, is its physical cure. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **A spring half** · **[idea]** · Below the turn the season is simply summer: there is no bud-break or leaf-out. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).
- **Falling as an event** · **[idea]** · Leaves vanish where they hang and are laid where the season's wind would have carried them: there is no fall through the air, no gust that shakes a tree bare, no rain day as a cause. (The travel routes between floor and canopy fly leaves, but as a transition grammar, not a season.) The carpet's stillness is José's decision and is not open. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md); see [`directions.md`](directions.md#distinctive-events-within-calm).
- **The season spectrum** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · José chose the spectrum, casualties and all ("i like the option of 'the spectrum', the one with casualties", 2026-11-09, from `test-gl/out/review/2026-11-09-item9-season-spectrum`). Six decisions are his, the largest being whether the season slider's summer half shows casualties on every floor look. A named gap beside it: the casualties land at the trunk's foot, where his photographs show bare soil under the crown *(from the bot's log, uncommitted)*. [`../model/season.md`](../model/season.md).

## motion

- **The incoherent band, driven by the wind** · **[accepted, unfinished]** · The fine drift is a phase advanced by hand or by a clock; when the incoherent wind band is built it was to become the driver of that phase. A prototype (2026-11-09, `test-gl/out/review/2026-11-09-item16-incoherent-band`) found two things: the flutter rock is already an incoherent forcing on orientation, one frequency, scaled by the wind, so the band is partly built; and a band driven by the gusts gives up the drift's exact periodic return, which is a law of [`../model/motion.md`](../model/motion.md) today: the canopy would return only to rest.
